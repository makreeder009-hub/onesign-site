Planning in Newcastle

will your sign need consent?

Much of the city centre, including Grainger Town, is a conservation area with many listed buildings, and illuminated signs there usually need consent from Newcastle City Council. We check what applies to your building at survey and can handle the application.

Questions
Do you offer free site surveys in Newcastle?

Yes. We are based in Team Valley, a few miles from the city centre, and we survey before we quote so the price is based on real measurements.

Can you make illuminated signs for a shop in Newcastle city centre?

Yes. We make halo-lit and face-lit 3D letters, backlit panels, light boxes and LED neon. In conservation areas such as Grainger Town illuminated signs usually need consent, which we can apply for on your behalf.

How long does a shop sign take?

It depends on the sign and on whether consent is needed. We give you a timescale with your quote, once we have seen the building.
