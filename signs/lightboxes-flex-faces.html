light boxes & flex faces.

Two ways to light a whole sign face. Light boxes are generally smaller, with acrylic faces lit from inside. Flex faces are for larger scale: a printed fabric skin tensioned across a frame, with no seams.

Questions

light boxes & flex faces, answered.

When should I choose a flex face over a light box?

Once a sign gets large, acrylic faces need joins. A flex face covers big areas in one seamless skin.
